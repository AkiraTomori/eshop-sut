The moderator should ask the question in the participant’s preferred language and record the response as closely as possible.

## Researcher Scoring Note — Do Not Show During Rating

For Phase 3:

- Odd items Q1, Q3, Q5, Q7, Q9: contribution = response − 1.
- Even items Q2, Q4, Q6, Q8, Q10: contribution = 5 − response.
- Add all contributions and multiply by 2.5 to obtain a score from 0 to 100.
- SUS is a system-level measure; do not interpret it as a percentage correct.
