## 3. Measurement Rules

Apply these definitions consistently in all five sessions:

| Metric | Rule |
|---|---|
| Task success | Completed = all three milestones reached independently; Partial = some milestones reached or direct guidance required; Failed = no meaningful completion, give-up, wrong end state, or 10-minute timeout. |
| Time on task | Stopwatch from “You may begin” until the correct QR is displayed, the participant gives up, or 10:00 is reached. Record `mm:ss`. |
| Error | A wrong action that moves away from the goal or requires recovery, such as opening the wrong event, choosing an incorrect control, invalid submission, or navigating to an unintended page. Repeated identical actions count separately. |
| Hesitation | A visible pause longer than 3 seconds or verbal uncertainty about the next action. One continuous pause counts once. |
| Moderator intervention | Any guidance beyond neutral encouragement. Record it separately; direct guidance makes the result no better than Partial. |
| SUS | Participant rates all 10 statements from 1 (Strongly disagree) to 5 (Strongly agree) after completing or ending the task. |
